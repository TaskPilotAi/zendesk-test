What should you report?

Use this form if you have experienced or witnessed any of the following:

📧

Suspicious Emails

Phishing attempts, misdirected emails containing personal data, or suspicious attachments.

📱

Lost or Stolen Device

A laptop, phone, USB drive or other device containing personal or company data.

🔒

Unauthorised Access

Someone accessing systems, files or accounts without permission or authorisation.

📄

Data Exposed Online

Personal or confidential data found publicly accessible online or shared incorrectly.

What happens after you submit?

Here is what to expect once your report is received

1

Report Received

Your submission is securely received and a support ticket is automatically created in our system.

2

Confirmation Email

You will receive an email confirmation with your ticket reference number within a few minutes.

3

Security Team Review

Our security team reviews your report and begins the investigation process immediately.

4

Response Within 72 Hours

In line with UK GDPR requirements, a team member will contact you within 72 hours with an update.
